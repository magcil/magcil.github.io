Projects

MagCIL participates in the following research projects.

Research Projects

Name Description Start End
DeepFake Awareness A public awareness platform on synthetic media, funded by the Greek Ministry of Digital Governance. It lets visitors experience AI-generated voice, music and video at first hand and test how well they can tell genuine recordings from synthetic ones, building practical deepfake literacy for the general public. 2026 2027
PHAROS AI Factory Greece’s national AI Factory, one of the first established under the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking, providing AI and high-performance computing infrastructure, services and expertise. MagCIL contributes to the Language & Culture vertical with: a platform for the analysis of Greek traditional music, covering genre, instrument and regional-style recognition with semantic indexing and natural-language search; sound event recognition services built on our deepaudio-lab platform; and song identification through audio fingerprinting, for monitoring music use in broadcast and public spaces. 2025 2028
DeployAI Development and Deployment of the European AI-on-demand Platform. Aims to design, deploy, and operate a fully functional AI-On-Demand platform (AIoDP) that promotes trustworthy, ethical, and transparent European AI solutions for industry and the public sector. MagCIL contributes audio AI services to the platform: music fingerprinting for song identification, speech recognition and audio event recognition. 2024 2027
MI-TRAP MItigating Transport-Related Air Pollution in Europe. An EU-funded project developing data-driven methods for monitoring and mitigating transport-related air pollution, combining environmental sensing with machine learning for source attribution and impact assessment. MagCIL contributes two real-time sensing components for the project’s environmental monitoring stations: soundscape quality assessment from continuous audio recordings, and vehicle counting from a single low-cost camera. 2022 2027

Past Projects

Name Description Start End
FaRADAI Frugal and Robust AI for Defense Advanced Intelligence. An EU-funded project focused on developing novel approaches, algorithms, and tools to strengthen the applicability and impact of AI in defence systems. MagCIL worked on semi-automatic data annotation, multimodal fusion for military applications, adversarial robustness in audio and few-shot learning. 2022 2026
Museek Automatic Music Monitoring of Public Performance. Develops a lightweight, device-friendly system that can automatically monitor and recognize music played in public venues such as restaurants, hotels, cafes, clubs, or bars. The project is a collaboration between MagCIL and GEA, the official music copyright collective management organization of Greece. 2022 2024
SmartSubs SmartSubs builds a realtime framework that analyzes recorded speech from theatrical plays, in order to automatically stream subtitles to smartglasses used by people with hearing problems. 2021 2023
Visor Visor (VIrtual coaching Services for OldeR adults) provides virtual coaching services for the elderly aiming to preserve the well-being of senior citizens towards a healthy lifestyle. 2018 2021
