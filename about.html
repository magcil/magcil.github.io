About us

The Multimedia Analysis Group (MagCIL) belongs to the Institute of Informatics and Telecommunications, part of the National Center for Scientific Research "Demokritos", the largest research center in applied sciences and engineering in Greece.

MagCIL focuses on multimodal machine learning, bringing together audio, speech, music, image and video analysis and generation. The group combines fundamental research, applied research through nationally and EU-funded projects, and teaching through the MSc in Artificial Intelligence co-organised with the University of Piraeus.
